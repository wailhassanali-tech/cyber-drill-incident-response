# Cyber Drill – Incident Response Exercise

## Overview

This repository documents a simulated cyber incident response exercise designed to replicate the challenges of managing a real-world security event. The exercise involved collaboration across multiple roles and focused on investigation, decision-making, communication, and coordinated response activities.

My primary responsibility during the exercise was serving as **Senior Decision Maker**, providing strategic oversight and supporting incident management based on information gathered by the technical investigation teams.

---

## My Role

As the **Senior Decision Maker**, I was responsible for:

* Reviewing investigation findings from technical teams.
* Assessing operational and organisational risks.
* Supporting containment and response decisions.
* Coordinating communication and incident priorities.
* Contributing to the overall incident management process.
* Assisting with final reporting and decision documentation.

---

## Exercise Objectives

* Simulate the response to a cybersecurity incident.
* Evaluate organisational decision-making under pressure.
* Coordinate actions across multiple participants and responsibilities.
* Assess potential business and operational impacts.
* Improve communication and incident handling procedures.
